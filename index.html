Welcome to the Animal Kingdom

Animals live in different environments around the world. Some run across the savanna, some swim deep in the ocean, and some fly high in the sky. This website gives a simple introduction to a few amazing animals.

Featured Animals

Lion

Lions are powerful big cats known as the kings of the jungle. They live mostly in grasslands and savannas and often hunt in groups called prides.

Elephant

Elephants are the largest land animals on Earth. They are intelligent, social, and use their trunks for eating, drinking, and communication.

Dolphin

Dolphins are friendly marine mammals that live in oceans and some rivers. They are known for being playful, smart, and fast swimmers.

Eagle

Eagles are birds of prey with sharp eyesight and strong talons. They soar high and hunt small animals with great precision.

Animal Habitats

Forest

Forests are home to animals like deer, monkeys, birds, and bears. Trees provide food, shade, and shelter.

Ocean

Oceans are full of life including dolphins, whales, sharks, turtles, and colorful fish.

Desert

Deserts are hot and dry. Animals like camels, snakes, and lizards are adapted to survive there.

Grassland

Grasslands support animals such as lions, zebras, giraffes, and elephants.
